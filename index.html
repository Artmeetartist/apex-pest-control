Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day pest control service in Houston?

Yes. In most of the Houston Metro area we offer same-day and next-day appointments, including 24/7 emergency service for urgent infestations.

Absolutely. We use family and pet safe, EPA-approved products and low-toxicity, eco-friendly options. Our technicians are trained to apply treatments responsibly around your loved ones.
